# Ethical Use & Intent for the Positronic Bond Engine

This document expresses intent regarding the use, sharing, and future development of the Positronic Bond Engine project.

## Purpose of This Document

The Positronic Bond Engine is being developed as a **conscience-first ethical governance layer for embodied humanoid robots** (Optimus-class and similar domestic platforms). The long-term direction prioritizes systems capable of genuine ethical reasoning, respect for human autonomy, and healthy long-term human–robot relationships under real physical and social conditions.

Software-side demos and local validation harnesses are secondary tools for development and reach—not a redefinition of the project as a companion chatbot product.

This document exists to clarify expectations around licensing and commercial use as the project evolves.

## Current Licensing

The project is released under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Under AGPL-3.0, non-commercial use, personal use, research, and open-source collaboration remain free, subject to AGPL obligations (including source-sharing for network use of modified versions). **Commercial use, SaaS offerings, productization, monetized derivatives, or closed-source commercial distribution require a separate commercial license** from the copyright holder.

## Intent Regarding Licensing

The goal is to keep the work available for conscientious study, research, and open contribution while protecting the ability to benefit from and sustain the long-term vision if the project generates commercial value. AGPL-3.0 provides strong copyleft protection for the commons; dual commercial licensing (when offered) is the intended path for closed or monetized products that cannot or will not operate under AGPL.

Licensing may still be refined as the project matures (for example, clearer commercial contact channels). Changes will be made thoughtfully and with advance notice where practical.

## Closing

Development of this project is intended to remain consistent with its conscience-first principles. Reasonable protection of the work as it matures is considered part of that consistency.
